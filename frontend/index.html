About

Throughline is a utility that creates a character to scene mapping from a submitted script. It is a reading aid for primarily English-language, Latin-script playtexts following recognizable Western dramatic conventions.

Detection is automated. Formatting, unnamed characters, and unusual structures can affect the result. When possible, Throughline will flag characters or scenes as ambiguous. Throughline can silently fail, producing a mangled or erroneous output; treat Throughline as a tool, and always defer to your own reading of the text.
